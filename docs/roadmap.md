# Roadmap

Everything that was on this page shipped in 0.1.0a5: alerts, missed-run detection,
Prometheus and OpenTelemetry metrics, sign-in with your app's users, user management in
the dashboard, schedule editing, maintenance windows, the dead-letter view and data
retention. See the [changelog](changelog.md).

What comes next is open. Ideas and use cases are welcome in the
[issue tracker](https://github.com/Pythondeveloper6/celery-manager/issues).
